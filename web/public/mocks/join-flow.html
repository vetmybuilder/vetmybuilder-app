Trade-offs at a glance

Option Stays on homepage? Friction for trades Build effort Vibe
A · Bottom sheet Yes Tiny link, can be missed Low Conversational, mobile-native
B · Centred modal Yes Tiny link, can be missed Low Compact, classic web
C · Role picker Yes Equal weight to homeowner Low Decisive, two-sided
D · Tabs in modal Yes Visible tab, can switch Med Unified, app-like
E · Dedicated page No (routes to /join) Strong banner up top Med Clear, SEO-friendly

My recommendation: Option C (role picker). It's the cleanest answer to your earlier "is this confusing?" worry — there's no chance a tradesperson clicks the wrong path. It also opens the door to a third option later (e.g. "I'm a friend recommending someone") without redesigning anything.
