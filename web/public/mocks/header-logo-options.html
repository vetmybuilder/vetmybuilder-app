Recommendation

Option A (wordmark only). The "VetMyBuilder" wordmark with the brand-coloured "My" is already distinctive on its own - that's what the mobile menu top bar uses. Removing the tile gives the header more breathing room and lets the wordmark do the work it's already doing well.

If you want to keep a graphical mark, Option E (vetted tick + roof) is the most on-message - it visually carries the "vetted builder" promise the brand is built around. Most distinctive at small sizes too (favicon, app icon).

Option B / C / D / F are safe but generic. Stick with A or E.
