Notes

  • A is the lightest touch - 8 line icons at 16% opacity. Easy to ignore, never competes.
  • B is dot-pattern + a few icons. Adds texture so the page reads "designed", not "blank".
  • C is the most expressive - rounded coloured medallions in the corners. Friendly, but loudest.
  • D is blueprint grid - clean, architectural, calm. Hints at construction without illustrations.

My recommendation: A as primary, with the optional dotted grid from B as a backdrop layer. Gives the page texture + theme without ever competing with the swipe deck or chat dock.
