Pick guide

  • A · Photo-led hero — most marketing-led. Big work photo + overlaid name. Drops you into "this is the trade brand" right away. Best if the gallery is the strongest asset.
  • B · Emerald gradient hero — bold trade-tone identity at the top. Most "obviously a trade page". Punchy.
  • C · Cream card stack — same language as /signup and the legal pages. Most cohesive with the rest of the brand. Reads like one consistent product.
  • D · Swipe-card preview — frames the page as "this is how homeowners see you". Strong reason for trades to keep the profile up to date.
  • E · Settings-style list — compact, info-dense. Best if "edit my profile" is the main reason a tradesperson visits this page (rather than admiring it).

My pick: D if you want to nudge trades toward keeping their profile fresh ("homeowners see this!"). C if you want it to feel like the rest of the new brand (cream cards, amber borders). E if utility wins.
