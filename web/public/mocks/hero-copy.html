Pick guide

  • A · Trades, made personal. — what we have. Soft, brand-led.
  • B · Trades, the personal way. — names the spam pain in the eyebrow.
  • C · Trades you'd actually trust. — community-led trust framing.
  • D · Sorted, the personal way. — UK-friendly outcome.
  • E · Find your tradesperson. — direct, with a 3-step verb eyebrow.
  • F · Trades, made simple. — confrontational eyebrow about lead-gen forms.
  • G · Your home, in good hands. — outcome-focused, reassuring.
  • H · No spam. Just trades. — punchy two-beat headline.

My pick: B if you want the homepage to lean into the anti-spam value prop (which the rest of the page already does), D if you want it warm and outcome-led, or stick with A if the brand-led version still feels right.
