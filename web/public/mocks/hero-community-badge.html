Pick guide

  • A · Avatar pile (white) — most familiar, lowest risk, plays nice with cream palette
  • B · Live ticker — best when stats actually move; needs a real-time feed eventually
  • C · Handwritten note — most on-brand, least loud, no count needed
  • D · Growth pill — analytical, only land it once "23 joined this week" is true
  • E · Circular stamp — most distinctive, biggest design swing
  • F · Avatar pile (dark) — A but bolder against the photo

My recommendation: Option A or C. A if you want it to read as classic social proof, C if you want it to feel like a personal note continuing the "made personal" tone of the headline. Both are easy to wire up - the count can come from /api/stats which already exists.
