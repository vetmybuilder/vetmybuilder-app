Notes

  • Indigo for chrome (hero band, eyebrows, stats numbers, primary action) - homeowner POV.
  • Emerald only for the trade's credibility markers (Verified pill, individual trade chips). They're describing the trader, not the page.
  • Amber for service-area chips - matches the homeowner pages' soft chrome accent.

My recommendation: B (drawer) on desktop because the chat already anchors the right column, so a drawer sliding in from there feels native rather than a stop-the-page event. Pick whichever fits your gut though.
