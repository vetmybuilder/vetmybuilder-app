Recommendation

E2 (house + verification tick badge) - reads instantly as "verified house" because the tick badge is a universal pattern (X / LinkedIn / Instagram). Strong at small sizes too.

E7 (two-tone) if you like the marketplace-bridging idea - house in the local brand colour, tick in the opposite. Subtle visual reminder that VMB serves both sides.

E1 / E5 / E8 are the cleanest "single mark" options. E3 / E4 / E6 are heavier but more distinctive.
