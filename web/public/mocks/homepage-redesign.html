What's the same across all four

  • Existing review ticker preserved on every variant - same data, same dot indicators, just slightly different surrounding treatment per option.
  • Three-step story: post a job → swipe matches → chat & hire. Same product, told four different ways.
  • "Why homeowners love it" always has the same three pillars: verified, neighbour-recommended, no spam. Just rendered with different visual weight.
  • Indigo brand on the homeowner-facing CTAs. Optional emerald accent on Option C's swipe-card preview.
  • Existing stats section ("communityMembers ≥ 50" gate) can drop in below the why-love-us pillars on whichever variant you pick - I left it out of the mocks for clarity but the data path stays untouched.
  • "How it Works" deeper page (current /how-it-works) can keep its longer-form story; this homepage redesign just teases it.

Slogans up for grabs

  • A: Finding the right builder should feel personal.
  • B: Skip the spam. Meet your match.
  • C: Match. Chat. Done.
  • D: Trades, the way it should be.

Or remix - "Trades, made personal" / "Like dating, but for builders" / "The personal way to hire a tradesperson". Tell me which lane and I'll wire one.

My pick

Option A (Personal journey) for the homepage main scroll - it's the most balanced for a returning visitor: clear slogan, immediate CTA, structured story, all four sections in roughly equal weight, easy to skim. Ports the existing review ticker cleanly.

Option B if you want more attitude / contrast against incumbents. Option C if you want the actual product visible above the fold (good for paid-acquisition pages). Option D if you're aiming at a slower, narrative read (best for an /about page).
